The Challenge

Navigating Legacy Friction in a Modern Market.

  • Outdated UI and branch-based dependency
  • Limited self-service capabilities
  • No fully digital onboarding experience
  • Missing essential features like: biometric authentication, instant payments, real-time interactions
  • Fragmented experience across channels
  • Intense market pressure from fintechs and expectation of younger demographics

"The bank risked losing relevance due to its inability to adapt quickly."

Project Methodology
01

Discovery

  • Market and competitor analysis for fintech benchmarks
  • Facilitated stakeholder workshops to align on vision and priorities
  • Identification of critical user journeys (Onboarding and registration, Account management & Payments and transactions)
02

Define

  • Creation of IA for mobile and web platforms
  • Designing end-to-end flows across key journeys
  • Prioritizing digital self-service, speed efficiency, modern UI and reduced dependency on physical branches
03

Design

  • Mobile-first approach, later scaling to web experience
  • Iterative usability testing cycles to refine logic
  • Focused on simplifying user flows and improving accessibility overall
  • Evolution into a robust, scalable banking design system
