Command Center Integration Monitor · OpenText

From integration noise to operational clarity

"The problem wasn't data. It was distance." Enterprise teams monitor thousands of trading partners, yet still had to connect the dots themselves when something broke.

ChallengeSignals were buried across enterprise, partner, protocol, and document layers, with no clear path from alert to root cause.
ApproachCreated a progressive-disclosure IA — enterprise → partner → protocol → document — that highlights deviations from expected activity, not just raw numbers.
OutcomeFaster detection, less manual investigation, and a system that helps teams ask the next question instead of hunting for the first one.

How I think

Make complexity easier to act on.

01

Understand

Talk to users, study the data, and map the system as it actually works — not as the org chart says it does.

02

Structure

Define the information architecture and decision path before touching a single screen.

03

Design

Turn structure into an interface that prioritises what deserves attention first.

04

Validate

Test with real users, refine the logic, and be honest about what isn't working yet.

05

Scale

Turn proven patterns into systems so teams can build faster and more consistently.
